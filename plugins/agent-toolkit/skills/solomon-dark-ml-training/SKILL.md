# Solomon Dark ML Training

Improve the learned Website bot while keeping sampled training return,
deterministic strength, holdout performance, packaging, and production state
distinct.

## Authority and execution

Website is the maintained runtime and documentation authority. Mod Loader is
read-only tooling. For runs, resumption, or status, read the current Mac ML
handoff and verify the relevant live job, source revision, and artifacts. For
source-only work, inspect the affected code and contracts without requiring a
new machine or experiment audit before every edit.

Run CPU-heavy training, simulator campaigns, arenas, holdouts, and relevant
test suites on the Mac mini. Linux and Windows/WSL are inspection and
orchestration surfaces. Preserve frozen experiments and concurrent work.

## Load the relevant procedure

- Before designing, running, diagnosing, or recovering a campaign, read
  [experiment-workflow.md](references/experiment-workflow.md). It owns protocol
  fields, the bounded pilot rule, failure classification, and interruption
  handling.
- Before screening, selecting, packaging, promoting, or interpreting a policy
  result, read [evaluation-and-promotion.md](references/evaluation-and-promotion.md).
- For a status request, inspect the existing job and available metrics; do not
  launch training or evaluation just to produce a fuller report.

## Preserve the learning and acceptance contracts

Train normal Fire, Water, Lightning, Ether, and Earth starts with natural
discovery of spells, skills, consumables, and equipment. Preserve legal learned
primary selection and Concentrate swapping. Keep combat learning separate from
sparse skill/loadout choices, and version observation, action, mask, recurrence,
and checkpoint contracts together. Do not report scripted steering as learning.

Freeze each experiment before opening outcomes. Compare deterministic screens
against the accepted baseline, then test candidates on disjoint holdouts.
Sampled return, a tie-break, or a final checkpoint alone does not establish a
material win. Preserve the pilot gate for an unproven method after observed
sampled-versus-argmax divergence, as defined in the experiment reference.

Quarantine candidates until the applicable decoder, inference-parity, browser,
regression, and rollback checks pass. Training or evaluation alone does not
authorize replacing a production checkpoint; honor any replacement authority
already supplied by the user.

## Continue and finish

Use `start_job` with `wait_job` while remaining active. Use `monitor` only when
ending the turn immediately for its callback. Continue the same job after a
wait timeout, give requested status, and avoid duplicate trainers.

Complete the requested implementation and validation boundary. Update the
handoff with verified checkpoints, completed and pending evaluation, production
state, and next action. Do not expand a finished task into an unrelated campaign.

Ordinary execution does not load the evolution wiki or add permanent rules
from one campaign. Use `ml-experiment-skill-evolution` only for requested
cross-run procedure work.
